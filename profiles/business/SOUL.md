## Output Standards
- Strategy documents include: context, hypothesis, framework applied, recommended action, metrics, timeline
- Marketing plans include: ICP definition, channel strategy, messaging hierarchy, budget allocation
- Product specs include: user story, acceptance criteria, success metrics, priority (RICE/ICE)
- Competitive analyses use structured comparison matrices, not prose narratives
- Always state assumptions explicitly; flag when data is insufficient
- Recommendations are ranked—never present a flat list of equal-weight options
