📕 The Agentic GameHub — Community Rules

Welcome. This is a space for current, future/aspiring game devs and hobbyists to meet and build — especially around Unreal, Unity, Godot, and LLM/agent workflows.

1. Be respectful. No harassment, hate speech, discrimination, or personal attacks. Critique the work, not the person.
2. Stay on-topic. Game-dev / tooling / AI-agent talk in main channels; everything else goes in #off-topic.
3. No spam or promo-dumping. Sharing your project is welcome — repeated unsolicited promo, link-farming, or @everyone/@role pings are not.
4. Keep it SFW & legal. No NSFW or illegal content.
5. Respect IP & attribution. Don't post others' assets/code as your own; credit sources.
6. Keep it constructive. Low-effort flaming, trolling, or thread-derailing gets cleaned up.
7. Mod calls are final. senior-mod / mod-lead decisions stand.
8. Disclose AI-generated content. If you share AI-generated assets, code, or writing, label it as such. Don't present others' or AI output as your own handcrafted work (extends Rule 5).
9. Reporting. To flag a problem or a member, use #reports (a confidential drop-box — only staff and the bot can read it) or DM an mod-lead / senior-mod. Reports are handled privately.

Enforcement: 1st offense = warning or 10-min timeout → repeat = human-mod review for mute/ban. Appeals: DM an mod-lead or the senior-mod.
