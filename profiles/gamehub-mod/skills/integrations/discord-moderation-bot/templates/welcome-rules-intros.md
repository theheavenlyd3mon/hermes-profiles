👋 Welcome to The Agentic GameHub

This is a place for game developers to hang out and build, whether you ship titles for a living, you're learning your first Blueprint, or you're wiring up LLM agents to do the boring parts. We cover Unreal, Unity, and Godot, plus the AI and agent workflows reshaping how games get made.

A few things to get you started:

- Read the rules below. They're short, and they're what keep this place worth coming back to.
- Drop a hello in #introductions. Who you are, what engine you're on, what you're making. It doesn't have to be polished. Most of us are mid-build and quietly embarrassed by our own code anyway.
- Pick your channels. Each engine has its own general, showcase, and help rooms, and #ai-agents is where the agent and LLM tooling talk lives. Stuck? Ask in your engine's help channel or in #help.
- Say something. A community is only as good as the people who post. A question, a screenshot of something broken, a half-formed idea, all of it belongs here.

Where everything lives:
  📣 Info — rules, announcements, resources, introductions
  💬 Community — general, off-topic, showcase, help
  🎮 Engines — Unreal / Unity / Godot (general · showcase · help)
  🤖 AI & Tooling — #ai-agents
  🛠 Dev — assets, snippets, debugging
  🔒 Staff — mod-ops, audit-review (you won't see these, and that's on purpose)

About the bot: Gamehub-mod keeps an eye on things. It triages reports, posts announcements once a human approves them, and handles timeouts. It won't chime into member channels to chat. Something off? Use #reports (a private drop-box only staff and the bot can read) or DM an mod-lead or the senior-mod.

That's it. Make something cool, and be kind to each other. 🎮

② RULES  (PIN in #welcome-and-rules — 12 clauses, incl. AI-disclosure + reporting)
```
📜 The Agentic GameHub — Community Rules

1. Be decent to each other. No harassment, hate speech, discrimination, or personal attacks. Critique the work, not the person.
2. Stay on topic. Keep game-dev, tooling, and AI-agent talk in the main channels. Anything else goes in #off-topic.
3. Don't spam. Sharing your own project is welcome. Repeated promo, link-farming, and random @everyone or @role pings are not.
4. No NSFW or illegal content.
5. Credit your sources. Don't post other people's assets or code as your own.
6. Stay constructive. Low-effort flaming, trolling, and thread-derailing gets cleaned up.
7. Mod decisions stand. When an mod-lead or the senior-mod makes a call, that's the call.
8. Label AI-generated work. If you share AI-made assets, code, or writing, say so. Don't pass off someone else's work or AI output as your own.
9. How to report. See a problem or a bad actor? Use #reports (a private drop-box only staff and the bot can read), or DM an mod-lead or the senior-mod. Reports are handled privately.
10. Put engine talk in its lane. Unreal questions go in the Unreal rooms, Unity in Unity, Godot in Godot. Use #general to compare engines.
11. No pirated or leaked material. Cracked engine builds, leaked SDKs, and ripped assets aren't allowed. Link to legitimate sources instead.
12. Everyone starts somewhere. Aspiring and hobbyist devs are welcome here. If someone's stuck, explain patiently. We've all lost an hour to a node that wouldn't fire.

Enforcement: first offense usually gets a warning or a short timeout. Keep at it and a human mod reviews for a mute or ban. Think we got it wrong? Appeal by DMing an mod-lead or the senior-mod.
```
