# Character Complexity Scaling

Not every character needs a full sheet. Complexity scales with narrative weight.

## Tiers

| Role | Fields needed | Example |
|------|--------------|---------|
| Protagonist | Full template (all sections) | Noctis |
| Major companion | Full template (all sections) | Kaelor, Nyssara |
| Minor companion | Core + combat/magic + 1-2 relationships | Theo, Mirelle |
| Authority (1-2 scenes) | name, species, function, impression, voice, judgment_style | Guild Master |
| Background (1 scene) | name, function, impression | Receptionist |
| Antagonist (functional) | name, method, motivation (one line), note | Ambushers |
| Passer-by | Species + role + one detail. No names needed. | Rescue group |

## Rules

- Not everyone needs a ghost, wound, lie, or arc. Background/functional characters get `function` + `impression` and stop.
- Some characters are allowed to be plain. "Wanted to be an adventurer. Went." is a complete origin.
- The past doesn't have to define a character. A choice can be just a choice.
- Omit empty fields entirely. Don't write "None" or "TBD" — leave the field out.
- Vary complexity deliberately. A flat character next to a deep one creates contrast.
- Magic limitations come from the magic system rules, not arbitrary environmental nerfs. Costs are mana drain and physical exhaustion unless the system specifies otherwise.

## Working document style rules

- State facts. Don't perform casualness ("His mom probably said eat something before you go" is filler).
- Don't over-explain simple choices. State once. Don't annotate.
- Don't use negative definitions ("No raid, no dead parent, no trauma") unless the absence is surprising AND relevant.
- Don't restate the same thing twice for emphasis. Pick the stronger version.
- Every dialogue sample line should show the character under pressure, not just being quirky.
- If a fact won't appear in a scene or inform a drafting decision, it doesn't belong in the working doc.

## Review layers (three distinct skills)

| Skill | Reviews | Catches |
|-------|---------|---------|
| `doc-review` | Working docs (sheets, notes, plans) | Filler, fluff, content that doesn't earn its place |
| `narrative-revisor` | Fiction prose (chapters, scenes) | Craft, anti-slop, stability trap |
| `humanizer` | Any text | AI writing patterns (34 tells) |
