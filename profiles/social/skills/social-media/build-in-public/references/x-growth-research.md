## Volume Benchmarks (Metricool, 23K+ accounts)

- Top accounts: ~95 posts/week (~13-14/day)
- Average account: 12 posts/week (~1-2/day)
- Build-in-public sweet spot: 3-6 posts/day (mix of originals, replies, threads)

## Content Performance Data

- Threads get highest algorithmic reach (hook → 5-7 value tweets → CTA)
- Failure stories get 2-3x more engagement than success stories
- Questions drive replies (algorithm loves reply density)
- Visual content (screenshots, GIFs, demo videos) gets 2x more impressions
- Revenue transparency posts build trust fastest

## Reply Strategy Details

- Target accounts 2-10x your size (not too big to be invisible, not too small for reach)
- Reply within first hour of their post (algorithm window)
- Add genuine value: insight, related experience, thoughtful question
- Never pitch in replies — relationship first
- 15-30 minutes daily on replies
- The algorithm "remembers" interactions — consistent replies to same accounts boost your visibility in their feed
