# X Premium Tier Selection for Build-in-Public

## Tier Comparison (as of 2026)

| Feature | Basic ($3/mo) | Premium ($8/mo) | Premium+ ($40/mo) |
|---------|---------------|-----------------|-------------------|
| Edit posts | ✓ | ✓ | ✓ |
| Longer posts (25k chars) | ✓ | ✓ | ✓ |
| Longer videos (3h, 8GB) | ✓ | ✓ | ✓ |
| Blue checkmark | — | ✓ | ✓ |
| Ads | Standard | ~50% fewer | No ads |
| Creator monetization | — | ✓ | ✓ |
| Grok AI limits | — | Standard | Higher |
| Reply prioritization | Basic | Greater | Highest |
| Radar Search | — | — | ✓ |
| Articles | — | — | ✓ |
| X Pro (multi-column) | — | — | ✓ |

## Recommendation: Premium ($8/mo)

For build-in-public users, Premium is the sweet spot:

1. **Blue checkmark** — credibility when starting from 0 followers. People click profiles with checkmarks more.
2. **Reply prioritization** — your replies show higher in threads of bigger accounts. This is the #1 growth lever for accounts under 5K followers.
3. **Creator monetization** — revenue sharing + subscriptions unlock at Premium tier. When you hit 5K+ followers and post threads, this becomes passive income.
4. **50% fewer ads** — your content gets more feed time.
5. **You have Hermes** — Grok's higher limits (Premium+) are irrelevant when you have a full AI agent.

## Why NOT Premium+ ($40/mo)

- $32/mo saved = funds your VPS + API costs
- Radar Search — you can search with xurl/web instead
- Articles — link to GitHub/blog instead
- Zero ads — 50% fewer is enough for growth
- Highest reply prioritization — Premium's boost is already significant

## Build-in-Public Content Strategy

### Daily Routine (45 min total, mostly automated)

```
MORNING (you, 5 min):
  - Check Hermes notifications
  - Reply to high-engagement threads
  - Tell Hermes what you're building today

AUTO (Hermes via xurl cron, 0 min from you):
  - 9 AM ET: Posts morning update (what you're building)
  - 12 PM ET: Posts midday progress
  - 5 PM ET: Posts wrap-up (what shipped, what didn't)

WEEKLY (you + Hermes, 30 min):
  - Friday: Hermes drafts weekly progress thread
  - You review and approve before posting
```

### Content Types (rotate)

1. "Built this today" — feature/screenshot/GIF
2. "Debugging hell" — honest failure story (these get MORE engagement)
3. "Lesson learned" — actionable insight
4. "Revenue/metrics update" — transparent numbers
5. Thread — deep-dive on a technical decision

### Engagement Strategy (15-30 min daily, YOU not Hermes)

- Reply to 10 tweets from other builders (#buildinpublic)
- Reply to 5 tweets from potential users (understand pain points, don't pitch)
- Engage with accounts 10-100x your size
- Add value in replies, don't pitch
- Auto-posting is fine. Auto-replying feels robotic and kills engagement.

### Peak Posting Times (2026 data)

- Secondary: Mon-Tue ~10 AM ET
- Weekend: Sat 8-10 AM, Sun 9 AM-12 PM

## Pitfalls

1. **Don't post generic AI content.** Build-in-public audiences detect AI slop instantly. Every post should reference something specific you built, a real bug you hit, or a genuine insight.
2. **Don't automate replies.** Auto-posting is fine. Auto-replying feels robotic and kills engagement. Hermes posts, you reply.
3. **Don't skip the manual phase.** Post manually for 2-3 weeks to find your voice before automating. The social profile's SOUL.md should reflect YOUR voice.
4. **Don't neglect engagement.** Posting without replying is shouting into the void. The 15-30 min daily reply routine is what grows followers.
5. **Don't post your VPS IP address.** Tempting to show off infra, but it gives attackers a direct target.
