# Suno Artist Branding — Dual Complementary Artist Workflow

The RIVEN × SOLA pattern for creating two artist profiles with
opposing but complementary aesthetics, shareable visual identities,
and a collab crossover project for Suno music generation.

---

## The Pattern

Create **two artist profiles** with deliberately contrasting aesthetics
that share a tonal universe. Then optionally define a **collab identity**
that bridges them.

| Dimension | Artist A (e.g. RIVEN) | Artist B (e.g. SOLA) |
|---|---|---|
| Gender | Male | Female |
| Genre | Trap Soul / R&B | R&B / Alt-Pop / Trap Soul |
| Mood | Moody, Noir, Introspective | Ethereal, Warm, Intimate |
| Era | Dark Minimal Cold | Warm Neo-Soul |
| Vibe | Late-night, rain-on-glass | Afternoon sun, incense haze |
| Palette | Black, steel blue, charcoal | Terracotta, cream, rose gold |
| Light | Cold blue backlight (moon) | Warm golden front-light (sun) |
| BPM | 70-85 | 65-80 |
| Key | Minor: Dm, Ebm, Fm | Minor: Cm, Fm, Abm |

## suno-ready.md — What To Include

1. **Style Prompt for Artist A** — Paints full sonic picture in [bracket] format:
   Genre, Mood, Era, Instruments, Vocal Style, Production, Dynamics Arc, BPM, Key

2. **Style Prompt for Artist B** — Same structure, contrasting palette

3. **Collab Style Prompt** — Hybrid of both worlds; "Dark Noir meets Warm Neo-Soul"

4. **Song Concept — Artist A Solo** — Full lyrical framework:
   - Hook concept (1 sentence)
   - Emotional core (2-3 sentences)
   - Verse/Chorus dynamic (section-by-section breakdown)
   - Lyrical snippet (3-6 stanzas with performance direction)

5. **Song Concept — Artist B Solo** — Same structure

6. **Song Concept — Collab** — Call-and-response structure:
   - Who opens, who enters where
   - Trading lines vs. full harmony
   - Overlap zones
   - Outro ownership

## grok-ready.md — What To Include

1. **Cover Art Concept** with:
   - Visual brief (how the two worlds fuse)
   - Composition & framing (crop, perspective, depth)
   - Colour palette table (element → colour → hex)
   - Lighting & mood (two-source lighting per artist)
   - Subject & detail descriptions (clothing, accessories, expression)
   - Textures & atmosphere
   - Typography overlay specs

2. **Grok Imagine Prompt** — Full ready-to-paste prompt for image gen

3. **Artist Physical Descriptions** (per artist):
   - Age/vibe, skin/complexion, face, hair, build
   - Signature style/wardrobe (detailed, repeatable)
   - Presence/mannerism notes
   - Constant accessories (jewellery, chains, rings)

4. **Grok Portrait Prompt** — Per artist, ready-to-paste

5. **Bio** — Collab story, tagline, hashtag

## Central Trick: Opposition as Identity

The power comes from clear, deliberate opposition:

- **RIVEN** = cold, dark, introverted, guarded, moonlight, 3AM energy
- **SOLA** = warm, soft, open, intimate, sunlight, golden-hour energy

Their collab identity then writes itself: *"Frost on a warm window"* /
*"What happens when two worlds that shouldn't fit somehow do"*

**Rule of thumb:** if you can't describe each artist's aesthetic in
3 keywords that oppose the other artist's 3 keywords, the contrast
isn't sharp enough.
