# 15 Rhyme Schemes for Songwriters

**Source:** ASCAP Career Development, Jordan Reynolds
**Loaded by:** `songwriting-and-ai-music` skill

---

## What Is a Rhyme Scheme?

The pattern of end-of-line rhymes. Notation: first rhyme = **A**, second = **B**, etc. Non-rhyming lines get their own letter.

---

## Rhyme Types (Strongest to Weakest)

| Type | Definition | Example | Prosody |
|------|------------|---------|---------|
| **Perfect** | Same vowels + ending consonants | lean/mean | Stable, resolved |
| **Family** | Same family consonants (plosives, fricatives, nasals) | crate/braid | Stable, soft |
| **Additive/Subtractive** | Same vowels ± a consonant | eve/believe | Near-stable |
| **Assonance** | Same vowels, different consonants | had/glass | Unstable |
| **Consonance** | Different vowels, similar consonants | scene/when | Unstable, edgy |

*Internal rhymes* occur within lines, not just at ends.

## 15 Rhyme Schemes

### Four-Line Schemes

| # | Scheme | Best For | Example |
|---|--------|----------|---------|
| 1 | **AABB** | Narrative verses, clarity | "Daniel" — Elton John |
| 2 | **ABAB** | Rhythmic flow, tension | "Level of Concern" — TØP |
| 3 | **AAAA** | Rap flows, hypnotic | "Memories" — Maroon 5 |
| 4 | **ABBA** | Building to a punch | "Crazy" — Willie Nelson |
| 5 | **AAAB** | Tension → release punch | "Smooth" — Santana |
| 6 | **ABCB** | Conversational, loose | Jonas Brothers |
| 7 | **AABA** | Refrain-based, classic | "Nile" — Beyoncé |
| 8 | **AABC** | Building to finish | Maroon 5 |
| 9 | **ABCA** | Circular, return to start | Matchbox 20 |
| 10 | **ABCD** | Free-flowing, no rhyme | "Buddy Holly" — Weezer |

## Strategy: Mix Schemes Between Sections

| Section | Recommended Scheme | Why |
|---------|-------------------|-----|
| **Verse** | AABB or ABAB | Clarity for storytelling |
| **Pre-Chorus** | AABA or ABBA | Build tension |
| **Chorus** | ABCB or AABB | Memorability, simpler |
| **Bridge** | AABBA or ABCD | Contrast, surprise |
| **Outro** | AAAA or ABCB | Repetition or fade |
