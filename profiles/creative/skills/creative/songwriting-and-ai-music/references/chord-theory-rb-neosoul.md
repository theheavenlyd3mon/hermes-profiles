# Chord Theory for R&B, Trap Soul & Neo-Soul

**Loaded by:** `songwriting-and-ai-music` skill
**Applies to:** RIVEN (trap soul, dark, minor) and SOLA (neo-soul, warm, major)

---

## R&B vs Neo-Soul Harmony

| | **R&B Chords** | **Neo-Soul Chords** |
|---|---|---|
| **Harmony** | Diatonic, functional | Extended, non-diatonic, borrowed |
| **Voicings** | Maj7, min7, Dom7 | 9ths, 11ths, 13ths, altered, slash chords |
| **Resolution** | Strong (ii-V-I) | Delayed or avoided |
| **Purpose** | Support vocal melody | Chords ARE the atmosphere |
| **Rhythm** | On-beat, lightly syncopated | Swung, off-grid, elastic |

**Golden Rule:** R&B chords in verses (support the vocal story). Neo-Soul in bridges/outros (build atmosphere).

## 7 Essential Progressions

### 1. The 2-5-1 (Jazz/Gospel Root)
```
Major: Dm7 → G7 → Cmaj7
Minor: Bdim → E7 → Am7
```
Resolution, finality. Foundation of neo-soul.

### 2. The 1-4 (Movement & Anticipation)
```
Cmaj7 → Fmaj7
```
With passing chords: `C → Bb7 → F → Cm7b5 → Ddim → C`
Heard in: "Redbone" (Childish Gambino)

### 3. vi–IV–I–V (Tension & Release)
```
Fm7 – C#maj7 – Ab – Eb6
```
Heard in: "Say So" (Doja Cat), "All of Me" (John Legend)

### 4. Circle of Fifths (Natural Flow)
```
I – vi – ii – V           (C – Am – Dm – G)
iii – vi – ii – V         (Em – Am – Dm – G)  ← neo-soul gold
```

### 5. 12-Bar Blues (Roots)
```
I – I – I – I | IV – IV – I – I | V – IV – I – V
```

### 6. Diminished Passing (Chromatic Tension)
```
C – F – F#dim – G              ← "Redbone" progression
Am – Em – G#dim – F – G#dim – Am  ← descending chromatic bass
```

### 7. Dominant Cycles (Movement)
```
C – A7 – D7 – G7 – C
```
Secondary dominants — each chord resolves a fourth up.

## RIVEN Signature Progressions (Minor, Dark)

| Progression | In Am | Mood |
|-------------|-------|------|
| i – iv – VII – III | Am – Dm – G – C | Brooding, cinematic |
| i – bVI – bVII – i | Am – F – G – Am | Modal, dark, unresolved |
| i – iv – v – iv | Am – Dm – Em – Dm | Minimalist, hypnotic |
| i – V/iv – iv – v | Am – A7 – Dm – Em | Secondary dominant tension |

**Bass:** Long-decay 808 follows chord root; sub-bass moves with harmony.

---

## SOLA Signature Progressions (Warm, Maj7)

| Progression | In C | Mood |
|-------------|------|------|
| Imaj7 – vi7 – IVmaj7 – V7 | Cmaj7 – Am7 – Fmaj7 – G7 | Classic neo-soul warmth |
| vi – IV – I – V | Am – F – C – G | Four-chord pop with soul |
| ii7 – V7 – Imaj7 | Dm7 – G7 – Cmaj7 | Smooth resolution |
| Imaj9 – IVmaj9 – ii7 – V7 | Cmaj9 – Fmaj9 – Dm7 – G7 | Lush, layered |

**Bass:** Warm, round sub-bass. Less attack than RIVEN. Pad-like float.

## Reharmonization Trick: R&B → Neo-Soul

```
Original (R&B):      Fmaj7 – Dm7 – Gm7 – C7
Reharm'd (Neo-Soul): Fmaj9 – D7(♯9) – Gm11 – C13(♭9)
```

**Quick substitution guide:**
| Original | Neo-Soul Sub | Effect |
|----------|-------------|--------|
| maj7 | maj9 or maj13 | Lush, open |
| min7 | min9 or min11 | Complex, jazzy |
| Dom7 | Dom9, 13, or 7(♯9/♭9) | More tension, bluesy |
| minor | m7 or mMaj7 | Darker, sophisticated |
